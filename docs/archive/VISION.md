## What FleaFind is

Switzerland's discovery layer for the physical secondhand economy — not competing with Ricardo (online trading) or Eventbrite (ticketed events), but filling the gap for physical, in-person, browse-and-discover flea markets. The business improves every week that: the database gets fresher and more complete, more users get reached and retained, and more external people see FleaFind as the Swiss reference for this space.

## Three layers
1. **Data Trust** — the foundation. Users come because the data is right; Google ranks because it's verified and structured; organisers pay because listings send real people.
2. **Retention** — the loop. The weekly email system is live, so FleaFind now has an owned return path; the list and engagement evidence are still too small to call the loop proven. Future market/place follows and change alerts should deepen this utility only after delivery and return behavior are trustworthy.
3. **Authority** — the brand. Once data is clean and retention works, FleaFind becomes the reference — press, backlinks, organiser trust, eventually paid subscriptions.

## Operating model
Solo founder as steerer, not operator. AI runs research, content, implementation prompts, verification triage; Delfim approves and decides. The database gets fresher without daily manual work; Delfim makes the calls that matter (region commits, page-type admission, pricing, direction). As of July 2026, this includes periodic external AI audits (the "Fable audit" process) as a standing quality-check practice, not a one-off.

## International expansion
Austria remains the most plausible first international market, but it is not scheduled. The former 3,000-monthly-visitor trigger has been retired: FleaFind has passed that traffic level without yet proving that expansion should outrank Swiss catalogue trust, retention, and operating leverage. A second country requires a fresh, explicit decision based on sustained Swiss demand and a repeatable maintenance model.

## Monetization path
Near-term: organiser featured/verified packages (~CHF 19/month). Secondary, at scale: AdSense (realistic Swiss ceiling ≈ CHF 200–800/month at full maturity — a floor, not a strategy).

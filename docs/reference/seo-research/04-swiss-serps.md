# What ranks in Switzerland — what we could see, 2026-09-18

The research agent for this topic hit Google's bot check and was stopped without a report (it did not bypass the check, correctly). What follows is a bounded look through **Bing** (`cc=CH`), done by hand the same day — a proxy for who is in the market, not for Google's positions.

## Who ranks for "flohmarkt zürich 2026" (Bing CH, top 10)

1. conviva-plus.ch — a magazine piece, "Flohmärkte Zürich [2026 Übersicht] – 15+ Märkte, Termine & Orte"
2. **swiss-markt.ch** — "Flohmärkte im Kanton Zürich", "42 Flohmärkte im Kanton Zürich. Vollständige und aktuelle Informationen: Termine, Öffnungszeiten…". Market pages titled *name – rhythm* ("Nostalgie Floh-Markt Worb – Jeden 2. Sonntag im Monat"). **Not in `competitors.md`.**
3. **fleafind.ch** — "Flohmärkte in Zürich und Umgebung 2026: Termine, Öffnungszeiten und Adressen. Jede Woche geprüft."
4. **pikitup.ch** — "Flohmarkt Zürich 2026 – Termine & Märkte im Überblick", with classifieds. **Not in `competitors.md`.**
5. stadt-zuerich.ch — the city's own page
6. trovas.ch, flohmarktkalender.ch (twice), fleafind.ch home, and a "Flohmarkt Schweiz / Flohmarkttermine 2026 / **2027**" page — the incumbents already carry next year in the title.

fynda.market is not in Bing's top 10 for this or for "flohmarkt heute" — expected: Bing had not been told about the site until IndexNow went in today.

## The finding that matters: fleafind.ch is still live and competing

"flohmarkt worb" on Bing: the organiser's Jimdo site first, then **fleafind.ch** twice ("Nostalgie Flohmarkt Worb – Alle Termine 2026"; "Flohmarkt Worb - 11.-13. September 2026"), then swiss-markt.ch, meine-flohmarkt-termine.de, trovas.

Checked directly: `https://www.fleafind.ch/de/schweiz/worb` answers **200**, indexable, canonical to itself, title *"Flohmarkt Worb 2026: Nostalgie Flohmarkt Worb – Termine | FleaFind"*. The home page too. The old site publishes the same markets, towns and dates as fynda.market under a domain Google has known for longer. For every query fynda ranks 4–6 on, fleafind is a candidate for the spots above it — the same content, twice, from the same owner. That is the most likely single reason for "page 1, no clicks", and nothing in the docs says what is meant to happen to the old domain.

## Title patterns the incumbents use

- `Flohmarkt {Stadt} 2026: {Marktname} – Termine | Brand` (fleafind)
- `Flohmärkte {Stadt} [2026 Übersicht] – N+ Märkte, Termine & Orte` (magazine)
- `{Marktname} – {Rhythmus}` (swiss-markt)
- `Flohmarkt Schweiz / Flohmarkttermine 2026 / 2027` (year pair, from September)

Ours since today: `Der Flohmarkt in Worb 2026 – nächster Termin 11. Okt` and `{Name} – {Venue}, {Town} – Termine 2026`. Comparable; the next date is the one thing none of them puts in the title.

## Not seen

Google's own results, SERP features (events carousel, AI Overviews, map pack) for these queries, Google Trends seasonality, search-engine share in Switzerland. Google blocks automated lookups; a person looking at google.ch on a phone is the honest way to get this, ten minutes for the ten queries in the original brief.

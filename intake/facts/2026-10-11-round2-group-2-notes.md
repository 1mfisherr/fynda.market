## Changes, contradictions, judgement calls
- flohmarkt-lenzburg-metzgplatz: from 2026 the market is organised by Melanie Masché (Flohmarkt_Lenzburg@gmx.ch), "ohne Beteiligung des Kramers". The website we list is still Kramer's page.
- flohmarkt-am-mauerpark-berlin: flohmarktimmauerpark.de looks stale, with no news since 2022. Recorded outdoor only.
- flohmarkt-biel: the city's flea markets run "in der Altstadt oder auf der Esplanade beim Kongresshaus". We list only the Esplanade.
- flohmarkt-buelach-stadthalle: there are 80 hall stalls at every edition. The May and October editions add outdoor stalls: 140 in 2026, 94 in 2027. I recorded 80. Setting was left blank because January and March are indoor only, while May and October are indoor and outdoor. January and March 2027 are cancelled for renovation.
- flohmarkt-rheinenergiestadion-koeln: the text says stalls are on the "Vorplatz", but the price line reads "Laufender Meter : 15,00 € (drinnen / draußen)". Setting could be outdoor or both, so I left it blank.
- flohmarkt-daglfing-muenchen: the market is on a "großzügiges Freigelände" and also has an indoor Antikhaus "auf dem Flohmarkt". Setting could be outdoor or both, so I left it blank.
- flohmarkt-alte-holzbruecke-olten: the venue is a roofed wooden bridge. I left setting blank (outdoor but covered).
- flohmarkt-citti-park-luebeck: setting was left blank because the page offers roofed spots ("überdacht möglich", a surcharge "lfd. Meter Dach").
- flohmarkt-adliswil: "über 30 Standbetreiber" is given for the Brugge- und Flohmärt together, so I did not record it as this market's stall count.
- flohmarkt-hansa-carre-bremen: the market runs here only from September to mid-April. In spring and summer the same Sunday market is on the Bürgerweide.
- flohmarkt-barfuesserplatz-basel: the 2026 dates end in September, with no market October to December. The Vorschrift says "Januar bis Anfang Oktober".
- flohmarkt-innenstadt-kiel: the web page still says "Die Termine für 2025 folgen". The linked PDF has the 2026 dates, the last on 4 October.
- flohmarkt-am-rathaus-schoeneberg-berlin: the seller-mix quote comes from the Marktordnung, which is dated "Stand 01.01.2020".

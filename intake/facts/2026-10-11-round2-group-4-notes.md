## The page contradicts or adds to what we have
- `liesberger-flohmarkt`: we list Mehrzweckhalle Seemättli (indoor). The 2026 registration form also sells outdoor pitches ("3 m aussen im Freien", "4m gedeckt") next to hall pitches. The market is really `both`. Setting was not in this batch's missing list, so this was not recorded.
- `marche-aux-puces-coupole-biel`: the CAJ page says "Inscription pour l'intérieur. À l'extérieur, aucune inscription n'est nécessaire." So there are stalls inside and outside, which makes it `both`. Setting was not in the missing list.
- `nord-flohmarkt-holstenhallen-neumuenster`: the organiser runs two Holstenhallen markets: a "Riesen-Hallen-Flohmarkt" in the hall (November, December and January dates) and a "Riesen-Super-Flohmarkt" on the Holstenhallen-Außengelände (the outdoor grounds). So it is outdoor in summer and indoor in winter, and no setting was recorded.
- `nostalgie-flohmarkt-lyssach`: the stalls are 6 m bays in the Landi-Agrola Einstellhalle (a covered parking garage). Up to 10 dealers with tall vehicles go "oben auf Deck" (on the open top deck). This is mostly indoor with a few outdoor stalls, so no setting was recorded.
- `nachtflohmarkt-markthalle-basel`, `nerdyartist-markthalle-basel`: markthallebasel.ch/flohmarkt names "Nachtflohmarkt" and "Nerd Yard" in its intro. But the only dates it lists are the Hallenflohmarkt (7/8 Nov, 5/6 Dec), the Musikflohmarkt (5 Dec) and the Vinyl Markt (6 Feb 2027). Neither market has an upcoming date there.
- `les-puces-du-cpo-lausanne`: this is the CPO community centre's own second-hand corner ("espace brocante", in the basement, every Thursday 10:00–18:00). It sells donated goods, so there are no separate sellers or stalls. Worth checking that it fits as a flea market.
- `nachtflohmarkt-rindermarkthalle-hamburg`: the page says "Private Ausstellerinnen & Austeller, Kunsthandwerker + lokale DesignerInnen", but its AGB allows only used goods from private households. Because the two disagree, no seller_mix was recorded.
- `nostalgie-flohmarkt-worb-bern`: the stall count of 40 is the cap on reservations ("Die ersten 40 Anmeldungen"). In July and August only half that number of exhibitors is possible because of the grain delivery.

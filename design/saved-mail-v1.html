How it works, and the calls I'd make

  1. One list per e-mail address, kept by us. Today the saved list is only in the browser. Signing up copies it to our database, and from then on, saving or removing a market on that phone updates it. On another phone, the link in the e-mail ("Your saved markets") brings the list there.
  2. Never an empty e-mail. If none of your markets is on that weekend, nothing is sent. A weekly market lover gets it most weeks; someone who saved two annual fairs hears from us twice a year.
  3. One e-mail on a Friday, not two. Someone who also gets "The weekend around Zurich" gets one mail with their markets first, then the area. Same list, same stop link.
  4. No confirm step, like the newsletter. A confirmation click loses about a third of the people who meant to sign up, so the newsletter has none; this follows it. The first mail says what they signed up for and carries the stop link.
  5. Same send budget. It goes through the Friday digest we already run, on the free mail plan (100 a day) — fine until ~80 people.

Was sich ändert

  1. The first market is visible without scrolling. Before, you scrolled past a hero, a search bar and chips to reach one.
  2. The search bar became a filter. Ort · Umkreis · Zeitraum. Nobody typed searches on the old site — 64 in 26 days.
  3. Cards show distance, size and indoor/outdoor. The three things that decide whether you drive there. We don't have size or indoor/outdoor yet — they get captured during the import.
  4. Freshness moved onto the card. "Bestätigt am 24. Aug". Nobody else in the category shows this anywhere.
  5. One list, grouped by day. Replaces the side-scrolling weekend row. Side-scrolling hides markets — a list doesn't.
  6. Category tiles moved below the markets. They were 1.8% of clicks on the old site.

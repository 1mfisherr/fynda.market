What changed

No search form at the top. The three-field card is gone — it stood between someone and the goods, and it is the pattern the weakest competitor uses. The place chooser sits below the markets, where it reads as "and now make it yours" rather than "fill this in first".

The claim is the first thing, and it is a number. "157 Flohmärkte, jeder Termin mit Quelle und Prüfdatum" is the trust signal, the head-term content and the sentence an AI answer can quote — one line doing three jobs.

Six markets, not thirty-eight. Two large, four compact, then a button. A nationwide list is useless to everyone individually; this is a taste, and the chooser is the way out of it.

The second visit is a different page. One tap is remembered in the browser — no account, same mechanism as Gemerkt. A directory that opens on your own town is a different product from one that opens on 22 cities at once.

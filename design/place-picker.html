Picking a place — three ways

Today "Where" is a closed dropdown of 148 towns sorted under their cantons, a separate round location button, a separate "When", and then a "Show 195" button. Four controls before you see a market. Each option below asks one thing and shows markets the moment it knows where you are. Towns and counts are real.

1 · Type a townOne box, like Booking's "Where". Type three letters, pick a town, you land on its page. "Near me" is the first suggestion, so there is no second button.

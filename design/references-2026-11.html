Design references, November 2026

Gathered 2 Nov 2026 for a change of look, before any mockup. Delfim has not picked a direction yet. One warning came first: Muzli (30 Oct 2026) says AI-made sites now cluster into "newspaper layouts with hairline rules", which is close to fynda.market today.

This year's trends

  1. Warm off-white, one bold colour. week.wild.plus/athens-26. Pantone 2026 is a soft white. Fits.
  2. Warm paper instead of pure white. anthropic.com. Fits.
  3. Mono letters for dates and labels. dylanbrouwer.design. Muzli: 23% of designer-picked sites. Fits: dates like ticket stubs.
  4. Colour per category. hurryupandhavefun.com. Risky: fights the photos.
  5. Serif headline, sans body. substack.com. The most copied pairing this year.
  6. One family, big scale. getnudge.info. Roughly what fynda does now.
  7. Handmade, anti-AI. lolilaboureau.com. Creative Bloq's 2026 list. Candid photos fit; collage would read as costume.
  8. Type as the picture. gt-mechanik.com. Wrong for lists.
  9. Motion and guided scroll. floema.com. Wrong on a phone.

Sites like ours, done differently

  1. Brocabrac, French flea-market directory: brocabrac.fr. Place, date, type in one row; "this weekend / next weekend" picks.
  2. Fever: feverup.com/en/zurich. Photo cards, short dates ("22 Nov – 14 Mar").
  3. The Skint: theskint.com. One line per event, a dry voice.
  4. Airbnb: search page. Map and list together, one badge per card.
  5. Brooklyn Flea: brooklynflea.com. Crowd photos; "closed for the season, returning April".
  6. Luma: luma.com/discover. Cities and categories as tiles with counts.
  7. Songkick: metro page. Tonight / this weekend / this month; lists under date headers.

Claude's bet

Warm paper instead of pure white, with one stronger colour still kept for dates; dates in mono; bigger real photos. From the sites: Brocabrac's weekend picks and Songkick's date headers. Next step: a before-and-after mockup of the home page in the direction Delfim picks.

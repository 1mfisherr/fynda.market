Was sich geändert hat

  1. Das Datum trägt jetzt. The day number is set at 44px and heads its own band, the way B did it. The time drops to a detail inside the row. 38% of clicks carry a date; that is what the eye should hit first.
  2. Fünf Farben, fünf Markttypen. Colour now encodes exactly one thing — what kind of market it is — as a rail down the left edge and a two-letter code, like a line on a transit map. There is a legend, so it is readable rather than decorative. This needs a change to BRAND.md, which currently reserves colour for dates and status and forbids category colour as fill. Your call: the rule as written cannot produce a coloured system.
  3. Fotos, an drei Stellen. A 74px thumbnail per row, a hero on the market page, and a small strip showing what the place actually looks like at different hours — the answer to „lohnt sich das?" that no amount of text gives.
  4. Abgesagt heisst grau. The cancelled row keeps its slot, loses its line colour, and its photo goes greyscale. The colour system does the work — no extra badge needed.

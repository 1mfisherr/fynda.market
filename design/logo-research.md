## Research that informed the work

- [Pentagram: Yahoo](https://www.pentagram.com/work/yahoo). The identity keeps the full wordmark and derives a `y!` monogram for favicons and social media. Relevant principle: carry recognisable letters and punctuation into the small mark.
- [Pentagram: Mastercard](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mastercard). Simplification for digital use, and testing the mark on light and dark backgrounds. Relevant principle: a simple silhouette should work beyond one colour treatment.
- [Google Search Central: favicon requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/favicon-in-search). Square aspect ratio, minimum 8×8 px, with a recommendation larger than 48×48 px; a stable crawlable URL and one favicon per hostname. Search appearance is not guaranteed. These source-image requirements are separate from testing legibility in a 16 px browser tab.

Before installation, refine the chosen mark into vector outlines, inspect raster versions at 16 and 32 px, and export SVG, multi-size ICO, a square PNG larger than 48 px, and a touch icon. Preserve the wordmark's full name. Colour-only recognition is insufficient; the one-colour version should still read.

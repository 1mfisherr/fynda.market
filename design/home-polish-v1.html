Home page polish, v1

The live English home page on a phone, today and polished. Nothing moves, nothing is added and no block is removed: the same page, tidied. Scroll inside each phone.

  1. First screen. The organiser line becomes a quiet underlined link instead of a second button; less empty space before the town search; the city chips a little smaller. Together they lift the first market about 90 px, so its date shows on the first screen of a phone.
  2. “Not near Zurich? Change”. On its own line, instead of breaking across two.
  3. Distances. “0.7 km” instead of “0 . 7 km”. A bug: the full stop was given a digit’s width.
  4. “All 76 this weekend”. A solid outline and dark text. The dashed grey one looks switched off.
  5. Newsletter. The fields are drawn like the town search above them: a line, not a white box. The town the page already knows is filled in.
  6. For organisers. No second grey box under the newsletter, just a line and a link. Today the organiser ask appears four times on the page; this makes the quietest of them quieter.
  7. Cancelled this week. The market is struck through, as the brand says a cancelled market should be.
  8. Country headings. “258 markets · 132 towns” at normal spacing (it was squeezed by the heading’s tight lettering, a bug), and a thin line instead of the thick grey band.
  9. Town list. Rows closer together: the Swiss list is the longest thing on the page, about two and a half screens. The biggest towns’ numbers in black: the orange is meant for dates and status only.
  10. Market types. The big coloured tiles become the small tags a market row really carries. The line under the heading talked about a “stripe” that rows no longer have.

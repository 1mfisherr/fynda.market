Findet der Markt wirklich statt?

What this is arguing

The claim is first, and it is a number. Trust signal, head-term content and the sentence an AI answer can quote — one line, three jobs. No search form above it: that card stood between a visitor and the goods, and it is the pattern the one competitor with no listings on its home page uses.

Six markets, not 39. Two large, four compact, one button. A nationwide list is useless to any single person; this is a taste, and the chooser underneath is the way out of it.

The chooser sits below the markets. Above them it is a form. Below them it reads as "and now make it yours".

The second visit is a different page. One tap, remembered in the browser — the same mechanism Gemerkt already uses. Distances come from the chosen town, so "In der Nähe" works without ever asking for a location permission.

Desktop, first draft

Measured at 1440×900. Today every page is the 720px phone column centred in white — nothing above 900px has ever been drawn. This is not a second design. It is the same departure board with the extra width spent on information rather than on air. Four changes, in order of how much they matter.

1. A wide wrapper (1080px) for lists and chrome; prose stays at 66ch.  2. The day becomes a column beside its rows, not a band above them.  3. The row gains columns — facts and freshness get their own — instead of stretching.  4. The market page splits, and the two buttons that are the entire funnel become a sticky rail.

1 · Home

The day number in a left column is the whole idea. At 1440px a full-width band leaves ~600px of nothing between “04 FREITAG” and “2 MÄRKTE”; as a column it becomes structure, it sticks while you scroll that day, and the rows finally read as a timetable. The row now has five columns — time, name, facts, freshness, photo — so the width carries the things a visitor decides on instead of a gap.

The fix that is worth the most. PAGES.md measured the Route button at 859px down the page — below the fold, behind the whole date list — while directions are 55% of every outbound click on the site. On desktop the two actions become a sticky rail that never leaves the screen, and the address, times and entry fee move up beside them. The left column keeps the reading measure at 66ch, so the description does not become a 1000px line.

Was das kostet

One new token (--content-max-wide: 1080px) and one @media (min-width:900px) block per component — the breakpoint already exists in tokens.css. Nothing above changes the mobile layout, the markup order, or a single URL. The row keeps the same DOM: the two new columns hold what .via and .fresh already contain, moved sideways at ≥900px instead of stacked. Guardrail 7 stays satisfied — the wide wrapper and the day column belong in base.css, the row columns in MarketRow.astro, the sticky rail in the market page.
